# Sprint: tell the owner when a pull request widens the impact

Declared 2026-11-27, before work starts. Closed round. Source: the owner's "ship it" on a
one-deliverable proposal, and their words that they are notified if a fix expands the blast radius.

## Deliverables

| ID | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| D1 | Story `impact-widened-01` is merged at version 0.48.0, the sealed suite and CI pass, and the released script reports on a real pull request. |

## Bounds

Review rounds: 1 independent review, 1 fix round. A change reaching 50% or more of live features
waits for the owner before the merge.

## Out of scope

Refusing a merge, a finer way of counting reach, recording the owner's decision.
